I’ve had the pleasure of mentoring and collaborating with a lot of talented students and postdocs. At LANL, mentorship can be a bit different than in academia or the private sector (formal mentors exist in the system, but day-to-day technical mentorship is often shared across a team), so this list focuses on people I worked with substantially. People for whom I served as the official primary mentor are marked with an asterisk (*). If I’ve missed you or you’d like an update to your “Now” line, please send me a note. Now = "the best of my knowledge as of Jan. 2026."
