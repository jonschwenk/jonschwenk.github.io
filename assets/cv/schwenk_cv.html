Research Topics as PI

•

Climate impacts on water resources and infrastructure. Led interdisciplinary assessments of climate-change effects on water availability, hydropower generation, and permafrost-related infrastructure risk for U.S. government sponsors.

•

Adaptive reservoir operations. Led development of a reservoir simulation and reinforcement-learning framework to evaluate optimal hydropower and water-management strategies under operational constraints.

•

Hydrologic change and river prediction. Developed and evaluated streamflow models that account for watershed differences and human influences. Collaborated on studies of Arctic streamflow trends, river-temperature prediction, ice-breakup forecasting, and habitat-relevant river conditions.

•

Integrated river and watershed data. Led development of VotE, a river-centered geospatial platform for integrating environmental observations and supporting watershed analysis and modeling. Created RiverPIXELS, an expert-labeled Landsat dataset for mapping river water and in-channel sediment.

•

Permafrost observations and mapping. Led development and evaluation of high-resolution models of near-surface permafrost, examining their transferability across landscapes. Led synthesis of near-surface permafrost observations through the CommUnity near-Surface Permafrost (CUSP) dataset, including an agentic AI workflow for source discovery and curation, to support observational comparison and model evaluation.

•

Satellite observation of water quality. Led development of geographically informed estimates of Chesapeake Bay surface-water properties by integrating MODIS imagery with curated in situ observations.

•

Field observations of Arctic and restored landscapes. Led field work involving permafrost coring, frost probing, soil sampling, and sensor deployment in Arctic environments, including installation of radar corner reflectors. Collected soil samples for wetland-restoration monitoring in the Valles Caldera in New Mexico.

Research Topics as Co-I

•

River and delta channel networks. Developed methods for extracting channel-network topology and inferring flow directions, and contributed to global hydrography representing river bifurcations. Collaborated on studies of channel braiding, coastal network loops, discharge partitioning, and network controls on Arctic delta lake dynamics.

•

Arctic river dynamics and permafrost. Investigated how permafrost influences riverbank erosion across spatial scales and how river migration interacts with floodplain permafrost, drawing on field observations and synthesis of river erosion and migration records.

•

Satellite mapping of rivers and sediment. Mentored development of graph-based and active-learning methods to identify water and sediment in multispectral imagery while reducing labeled-data requirements.

•

River dynamics and biogeochemical processes. Collaborated on studies linking river migration and delta morphology with floodplain carbon burial, microbial communities, and nitrate retention.

•

Climate-ecology model integration. Co-led development of methods linking Earth system and ecological models across spatial scales to investigate mosquito population responses to climate change. Mentored predictive modeling of mosquito dynamics using climate-model output.

•

Societal instability and event forecasting. Integrated heterogeneous datasets with event records to model regional instability in Pakistan. Leading collaborative research on causal-inference approaches to conflict forecasting.

•

Sediment transport in delta wetlands. Mentored students in field investigations of sediment transport and accumulation in the resacas and adjacent alluvial basins of the Rio Grande Delta.

•

Scientific data standards for energy systems. Developed and tested a schema-generation workflow for MG-RAVENS, supporting consistent data exchange among microgrid and power-grid modeling tools in a DOE multi-laboratory collaboration.
